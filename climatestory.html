01 — A PLANET IN BALANCE

Everything is connected.

Earth's atmosphere, oceans, forests, ice and living organisms form a complex system.

🌳

Forests

Forests store carbon, support biodiversity and influence water cycles.

🌊

Oceans

Oceans absorb heat and carbon dioxide and influence weather patterns.

❄️

Ice

Glaciers and ice sheets store freshwater and influence Earth's climate.

02 — THE CHANGE BEGINS

Human activity is changing the system.

Energy production, transportation, industry and land-use changes affect greenhouse gas levels.

⚡

Energy

Energy production is a major source of greenhouse gas emissions.

🚗

Transport

Road, air and sea transport contribute to greenhouse gas emissions.

🏭

Industry

Industrial processes consume energy and natural resources.

🌾

Agriculture

Agriculture contributes methane and nitrous oxide emissions.

03 — THE INVISIBLE BLANKET

Understanding the greenhouse effect.

Certain gases in the atmosphere trap some outgoing heat. Human activities increase their concentration.
